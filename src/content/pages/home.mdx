# Welcome to the Free to Set Free Foundation!

We are a faith-based movement empowering families to break free from debt and build lasting spiritual and financial freedom. We have a heart to serve our community and make a difference.

<img class="content-photo" src="/images/johnny-and-lijah-cutting-boards.jpg" alt="Johnny and Lijah cutting boards" />

## Our Mission

Our mission is to provide faith-based financial literacy education and housing assistance through debt management services for underserved communities across Northeastern Ohio.
