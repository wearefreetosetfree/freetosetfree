## About Us

Free to Set Free Foundation is a locally operated, Spirit-led nonprofit based in Northeastern Ohio.

We are community-driven, financially independent, and led by individuals who understand real-life challenges.

Our leadership is committed to serving others and passing on the blessings we've received.

<img class="content-photo" src="/images/mr-flero-and-johnny-on-the-roof.jpg" alt="Mr. Flero and Johnny on the roof" />

## Our Focus

We exist to share God's love by helping individuals and families break free from the burden of debt.

Through faith-based financial education, community partnerships, and practical support, we empower people to grow spiritually, achieve financial freedom, and help others do the same.

<img class="content-photo" src="/images/amery-steve-and-washer.jpg" alt="Amery, Steve, and washer" />

## A Different Kind of Giving

We believe a committed community can accomplish extraordinary things.

Our model combines direct financial support (grants), education, and a cooperative, community-driven approach.

We eliminate unnecessary overhead, address the root problem of debt, and create sustainable, multiplying impact.

> This isn't charity. It's transformation.

### Our Core Values

- **Unity** – We work together as one body
- **Love** – We serve with compassion and humility
- **Service** – We act, not just speak
- **Spirituality** – Christ-centered in all we do

## A Biblical Vision for Freedom

Scripture reminds us that debt can become a form of bondage.

We believe God calls people to live in freedom, to help one another, and to build strong families and communities.

By relieving debt burdens, we help restore stability, dignity, and hope.

## A Vision for the Future

Imagine a community where families are debt-free, generosity is multiplied, and lives are transformed through faith.

When you give, your impact is direct, your support is visible, and your generosity creates lasting change.

**Join us. Be part of something eternal.**
